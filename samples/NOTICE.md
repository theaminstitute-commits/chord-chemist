# Samples

`jazz-guitar/40.mp3` … `79.mp3` (file name = MIDI note, E2–G5) are the "Electric Guitar (jazz)"
instrument from the FluidR3 GM SoundFont by Frank Wen, as rendered to per-note MP3s by the
midi-js-soundfonts project: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
(`https://gleitz.github.io/midi-js-soundfonts/FluidR3_GM/electric_guitar_jazz-mp3/<note>.mp3`).
